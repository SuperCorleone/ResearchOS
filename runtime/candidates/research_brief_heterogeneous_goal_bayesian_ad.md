# Research Brief: Heterogeneous-Goal Bayesian Adaptation for MAPE-K AI Workflows - **Proposal ID**: `heterogeneous_goal_bayesian_ad` - **Direction**: self-adaptive-systems - **Gate**: `pass` (total=12/15, repeat_degree=`low`) - **Gate reasons**: total=12 >= 9 ## Research Question Can Bayesian model averaging over multiple candidate system models improve adaptation quality in MAPE-K-style AI workflows when the controller must satisfy heterogeneous goals such as hard thresholds and optimization objectives simultaneously? ## Scores | Dimension | Score (0-3) | |---|---| | significance | 3 | | novelty | 2 | | differentiation | 2 | | feasibility | 2 | | falsifiability | 3 | | **total** | **12 / 15** | ## Innovation Claim - **Problem gap**: Existing methods fail because they typically assume a single selected system model and often optimize for one requirement type at a time, which can produce overconfident adaptation decisions when runtime uncertainty and mixed hard-threshold/optimization goals coexist. - **Our delta**: We propose extending Bayesian model averaging within a MAPE-K adaptation loop to combine multiple candidate system models while evaluating heterogeneous goals jointly, rather than committing to one model or one goal formulation. - **Why it matters**: Which improves adaptation quality and decision stability under runtime uncertainty, model-selection ambiguity, and conflicting heterogeneous objectives. ## Falsification Design - **Hypothesis**: In MAPE-K AI workflows with both hard threshold constraints and optimization objectives, a Bayesian model averaging controller over multiple candidate system models achieves higher constraint satisfaction and better overall utility than a single-best-model controller under runtime uncertainty. - **Baseline**: Single-model MAPE-K controller that selects the maximum-posterior (or best-fit) system model and plans adaptations using that model alone. - **Metric**: Primary: constrained utility score = mean normalized optimization utility with zero credit on any episode violating hard thresholds; Secondary: threshold violation rate and utility variance across runs. - **Failure condition**: BMA improves constrained utility by less than 2% over the single-model baseline or fails to reduce threshold violation rate by at least 5% relative, across the main uncertain-runtime evaluation setting. - **Expected signal**: At least +5% constrained utility and lower run-to-run utility variance than the single-model baseline. - **Min budget ratio**: 0.20 ## Knowledge Context 1. ## [concepts/taming_model_uncertainty_in_self_adaptive_systems_using_baye.md] (score: 0.7471) 2. code_refs: -
